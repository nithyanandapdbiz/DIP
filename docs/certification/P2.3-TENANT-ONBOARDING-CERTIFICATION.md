## 6. The one divergence from the brief, and why

The brief directed that Platform Core be added to **document 24 (Platform Intelligence)**. It was not, and [ADR-0021](../adr/ADR-0021-platform-core-bounded-context.md) records the reasoning.

**Document 21 already owns** *"tenant identity, onboarding, provisioning, state transitions, suspension, and offboarding."* **Document 08 already owns** *"trust boundaries, identity, authentication, authorisation."* **Document 24 owns** analytics and intelligence, and none of P2.3.

Placing Platform Core in 24 would have created **two owners for onboarding and two for authentication** — the precise anti-duplication breach the topic-ownership contract exists to prevent, and the architectural drift the brief's own governance rules prohibit. It would also have made document 24 answer two unrelated questions, which is the navigability defect the small canonical set exists to avoid.

**Every hard constraint of the brief is satisfied**: no document 26, no seventh capability, no third plane, no fourth Platform Service, Platform Core inside the Intelligence Plane, certified baseline intact.

**Reversal path, if the instructed placement is preferred.** It requires a superseding ADR recording that onboarding and authentication will have two documented owners, an impact analysis covering the topic-ownership contract, and re-certification of the M2.5a baseline because document boundaries would change. That is available; it is simply not free, and the cost is recorded here rather than discovered later.

## 7. Certification

> ### P2.3 architecture is CERTIFIED.

Ten gating checks green. Ten proofs recorded and replayed. Twenty-five documents, three Platform Services, six capabilities, two deployable runtimes. Zero orphans, zero duplicate topic owners.

**What this does not assert:** that onboarding works, that generation is deterministic, that registration is replay-safe in practice, or that sovereignty holds in code. **None is implemented.** §5 states exactly what implementation must produce, and all of it currently reports `NOT MEASURED`.
